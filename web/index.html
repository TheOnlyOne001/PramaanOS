How it works

Four steps turn a punch at the gate into a record that neither side can rewrite.

  1. 1

    Signed at the source

    The gate device, the supervisor's request and the camera box each sign their event with Ed25519 before it is stored.

  2. 2

    Hash chain and a daily root

    Every event carries the hash of the one before. A Merkle root is published each day, so a single edited row breaks the chain.

  3. 3

    A receipt in her language

    The worker gets a WhatsApp-style receipt in Tamil, Hindi, Odia or English, and her copy is checked against the employer's.

  4. 4

    Engines work from the record

    Pay, overtime caps, fairness checks, skill credentials and the SDG 8 scorecard are computed from the same signed events.

What it does

  • Fair Pay

    Deterministic pay from signed hours: overtime at 2×, a 144 h quarterly cap and nine fairness checks, explained in her language.

  • Live tamper test

    Edit a punch straight in SQLite and both apps flag it within seconds. Her payslip is held until it is resolved.

  • Anonymous voice

    Blind-signature tokens and an identity-free relay. A theme reaches management only at three independent reports.

  • Skill passport

    Production data issues a signed, W3C-VC-shaped credential. Any employer can verify its QR offline in the browser.

  • Edge privacy

    The camera box sends events and counts only. Anything that looks like a face, image or identity is rejected.

  • SDG 8 scorecard

    Live indicators for targets 8.2 to 8.8, each computed from the signed ledger and linked to its evidence.

The moment a record is edited

One SQL update to Meena's punch-out. The console raises an integrity alert, and her phone shows that her receipt disagrees.

Try the 3-minute demo

Open both apps side by side. The console's 🧪 Demo panel drives each step.

Start in the console Meena's app
  1. Punch in. Meena punches in and gets a receipt in Tamil.
  2. Overtime. Her supervisor asks for 2 h. The console warns that co-workers are near the cap. She accepts by voice, and her consent is signed.
  3. Live tamper test. Press “Tamper” in the console. The chain, the daily root and her receipt all disagree, and her payslip is held.
  4. Anonymous report. She reports that drinking water runs out. Corroborated reports reach the console with no identity attached.
  5. Skill credential. Her quality data issues a credential, and anyone can verify its QR offline.
  6. SDG 8 scorecard. Every indicator links back to its signed evidence.

SDG 8 targets we measure

  • 8.2Productivity: line efficiency
  • 8.3Formalisation: workers holding their own signed record
  • 8.5Minimum wage and equal pay
  • 8.6Skills: credentials per worker-year
  • 8.7No forced labour: overtime with signed consent
  • 8.8Safe work: near-misses logged, reports answered

Red lines: what we refuse to build

Records protect workers only if the system cannot be turned into surveillance.

  • ✕ Face recognition for discipline
  • ✕ Emotion recognition
  • ✕ Toilet-break tracking
  • ✕ Keystroke logging
  • ✕ Individual leaderboards
  • ✕ Union-activity monitoring
